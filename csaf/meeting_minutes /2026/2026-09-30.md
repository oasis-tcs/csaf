### GitHub Issues & Pull Requests

- Issue [#1616](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1616)
  - Thomas Schmidt asked whether the free-text details field used for publisher and involvement entity contacts, should be covered by the existing informative spelling and grammar tests and permit GFM.
  - Stefan Hagen questioned the value of spelling and grammar checks for short entries such as phone numbers and contact instructions. Thomas Schmidt used a Cisco support contact example to discuss formatting separate emergency and non-emergency instructions. Sergii Demianchuk posted an example on the issue. Thomas Schmidt explained that the specification lists the fields that permit GFM. Individual documents carry no separate format flag, and consumers can fall back to plain text.
  - Sonny van Lingen asked whether the formatting added enough value, given that the contact information would still be free text. He also asked whether relying on the GitHub dialect was necessary. Thomas Schmidt noted that CSAF already uses GFM and that it is widely supported. Stefan Hagen cited GFM's experience with hostile input, raised the risk of adding unrelated content to the field, and asked for a concrete example.
  - More time is needed to consider the proposal. The TC deferred the decision to a future meeting. An example will be prepared. No motion was set.
- Issue [#1564](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1564)
  - Thomas Schmidt proposed reserving test preset namespaces based on RFC 2606 and reserving a namespace for the TC's own use.
  - Thomas Schmidt moved to reserve `test`, `example` and `invalid` under both `x_` and `org_`, and to reserve `csaf` under those prefixes for the CSAF TC. Stefan Hagen seconded. No objections were raised. The motion carries.
- Issue [#1553](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1553) and PR [#1552](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/pull/1552)
  - Thomas Schmidt presented the Global CVE (GCVE) application to the Registry for Vulnerability ID Systems for CSAF (RVISC). The editors had reviewed the request. In response to Sonny van Lingen, Thomas Schmidt confirmed that this RVISC submission contained sufficient information to be handled by the TC. 
  - Sonny supported registration.
  - Thomas Schmidt proposed replacing the positive lookahead used to enforce the identifier length with explicit `minLength` and `maxLength` fields so that validators could check the length before evaluating the regular expression. He also proposed excluding positive and negative lookaheads from registry patterns. The technical changes would be handled separately from the registration motion. Thomas Schmidt confirmed that, once approved, the editors would work with the change controller to complete the registration.
  - Christoph Plutte moved to add GCVE to RVISC. Thomas Schmidt seconded. No objections were raised. The motion carries.
