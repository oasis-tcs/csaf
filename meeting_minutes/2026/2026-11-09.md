#### Issue [#1727](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1727) and PR [#1753](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/pull/1753) — TLP mapping links

- The comment proposed deleting the sentence requiring satisfaction of the normative requirements given below in the
  CVRF-to-CSAF converter conformance clause. Thomas Schmidt initially proposed retaining the sentence because it also
  covered the mapping tables outside the numbered lists.
- Christian Banse asked for clarification of both the process and the text, noting that the connection to the tables was
  difficult to follow. Participants discussed the normative status of the TLP mapping tables and whether adding explicit
  requirements could be interpreted as a material change.
- The TC settled on adding parenthetical **“see also” links to the relevant tables**, retaining the existing requirement.
  The same clarification would be applied to the corresponding CSAF 2.0-to-2.1 converter clause for consistency.
  The change was prepared during the meeting in PR [#1753](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/pull/1753).
- Thomas Schmidt moved to accept PR [#1753](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/pull/1753) into CSAF 2.1 as the comment resolution,
  mark the comment **Completed**, and classify the changes as **non-material**. Christian Banse seconded.
  No objections, further discussion or concerns were raised. **The motion carried by unanimous consent.**

### Request for a Special Majority Ballot on CSAF 2.1 CS01

Thomas Schmidt moved to request a ballot to promote **Common Security Advisory Framework Version 2.1, Committee
Specification Draft 04**, with the non-material changes from editor revision
PR [#1747](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/pull/1747), to **Common Security Advisory Framework Version 2.1,
Committee Specification 01**. The motion also tasked the editors to work with the TC administrators to facilitate the
ballot and make any non-material changes needed, including non-material changes to the comment log requested by the
TC administrators.

Sonny van Lingen and Christoph Plutte seconded. No objections, further discussion or concerns were raised.
**The motion carried by unanimous consent.**

The draft was to be made available after the meeting at the
[CSAF 2.1 CS01 release-candidate tag](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/releases/tag/csaf-2.1-cs-01-20261109-rc1).
The submission would include the resolved comment log, a permanent link to these minutes, the approval motion and draft
reference, and a comparison showing the changes since CSD04.

- Justin Murphy agreed to submit the ballot request that evening after the approved changes were merged.
  Thomas Schmidt would confirm readiness so that Justin could contact the TC administrators.
- The planned opening was **00:00 UTC on November 10**, with a **seven-calendar-day voting period**, intended to finish
  before the November 18 TC meeting. The TC administrators would issue the formal ballot and its schedule.
- Thomas Schmidt explained that approval as a Committee Specification requires a **Special Majority Vote conducted by
  the TC administrators**, rather than an approval vote within the TC meeting.
- For the **ten eligible voters** identified during the meeting, approval requires **at least seven Yes votes and no more
  than two No votes**. Abstentions are permitted but do not contribute to the affirmative threshold. These numbers are
  consistent with the [OASIS Special Majority rules](https://docs.oasis-open.org/TChandbook/Reference/Voting.html).
- All voting members were asked to cast their votes promptly once the ballot opened and within the seven-day window.
