### Full Majority Vote on standing rule SR2BPWE

Thomas Schmidt proposed adopting
[standing rule SR2BP](https://www.oasis-open.org/policies-guidelines/document-life-cycle-best-practices/#SR2BP), extended to
editors as well as chairs, under the identifier **SR2BPWE**.

Sonny van Lingen asked whether the rule concerned opening public reviews for work already approved as a CSD, CND or TRD,
rather than approving the draft itself. Thomas Schmidt confirmed that scope. The TC agreed to use **“chair(s) or editor(s)”**
so that either could initiate the public review independently.

The extended text submitted to the Full Majority Vote was:

> As a standing rule, a TC may approve the chair(s) or editor(s) to open new public reviews with each CSD, CND, TRD release,
> on a regular basis, or at their discretion, without additional motions, calls for consent, or ballots.
> A Full Majority Vote is required to enact this standing rule.

### Further GitHub Issue Discussion

#### Issue [#1510](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1510) — Infer the CWE version for CSAF 2.0 test 6.1.11

- **Background:** CSAF 2.0 does not identify a CWE catalog version explicitly. Validators using the latest CWE catalog can
  therefore reject an older advisory that was valid when published if CWE identifiers or names have since changed.
  CSAF 2.1 addresses this by specifying the CWE version.
- The proposed CSAF 2.0 implementation uses `$.document.tracking.current_release_date` to select the latest CWE catalog
  available at that date, rather than always validating against the newest catalog.
- Sonny van Lingen and Christian Banse asked whether this would change the CSAF 2.0 specification or confer normative
  authority on a particular implementation. Thomas Schmidt clarified that the intent was to recognize the proposed
  approach as conformant implementation guidance and document it in the known issues list, without changing the prose or
  issuing an addendum. Vijay Sarvepalli also supported documenting the limitation in the known issues list.
- Thomas Schmidt moved to accept the suggested implementation as a conformant and recommended implementation for CSAF 2.0
  and add it to the [known issues list](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/blob/master/csaf_2.0/known_issues.md).
  Sonny van Lingen and Christian Banse seconded. No objections, further discussion or concerns were raised.
  The motion carried by unanimous consent
  ([decision recorded on the issue](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1510#issuecomment-6322638885)).

#### Issue [#1691](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1691) — `initial_release_date` newer than revision history in non-draft documents

- Thomas Schmidt proposed a mandatory test for non-draft documents to prevent `initial_release_date` from being later than
  the oldest revision-history entry. The discussion included expressing the rule as requiring the initial release date to
  match the date of version `1` or `1.0.0`.
- Sergii Demianchuk, Vijay Sarvepalli and Sonny van Lingen supported making this a mandatory test. The remaining question
  was whether implementation and review could be completed before that evening's CSD04 submission.
- The TC considered requiring the change and delaying CSD04 if necessary, deferring it to a possible later CSD, or letting
  the editors include it only if time permitted. Vijay Sarvepalli recommended the third option and offered assistance.
  Thomas Schmidt noted that a reviewer would be needed that evening.
- Thomas Schmidt moved to accept the change as a mandatory test in CSAF 2.1 and include it in CSD04 **if time permitted;
  otherwise, leave it out**. Sergii Demianchuk seconded. No objections, further discussion or concerns were raised.
  The motion carried by unanimous consent
  ([decision recorded on the issue](https://github.com/oasis-tcs/csaf/issues/1691#issuecomment-6322848443)).
  CSD04 submission would proceed without being delayed for this test.
